Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026 · NexivoReach private beta

NexivoReach (“we”, “us”) helps B2B sellers find and contact prospective buyers. This policy explains what we collect during the private beta and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account data — Google account email, name, and profile photo when you sign in.
  • Workspace data — company profiles, product catalogs, ICP settings, leads, outreach drafts, and activity logs you create in the product.
  • Connected Google services — if you connect Gmail and/or Google Sheets, we store OAuth tokens so we can send mail from your mailbox and read/write spreadsheets you authorize in your Drive.
  • Usage metrics — daily counts of hunts, catalog extracts, draft prepares, and sends for rate limits and operator dashboards.
  • Support — tickets and appeal messages you submit.

How we use data

  • To run discovery, scoring, draft generation, sending, and Sheets sync that you request.
  • To enforce invite-only access, usage caps, and account suspensions.
  • To operate the admin console for invited operators of this deployment.

AI and third parties

Prospect research and drafting may send relevant product/ICP/lead context to AI providers we configure for this deployment (for example Groq). Web search providers may receive search queries. We do not sell your customer lists.

Your Google data

Gmail and Sheets access is used only to perform actions you initiate (send outreach, sync products/leads). You can disconnect Gmail or Sheets in Workspace → Connect. Disconnecting removes our stored tokens for that service. Google sign-in uses basic profile scopes only until you explicitly connect workspace services.

Retention

Data is kept while your account is active on this deployment. Operators may delete accounts or clear leads on request. Appeal and support tickets may be retained for review history.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use in-app Support (or the suspension appeal form if your account is paused), or email the operator who invited you to this beta.
