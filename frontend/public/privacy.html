Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026 · NexivoReach

NexivoReach helps B2B sellers find buyer accounts and send outreach from their own Gmail. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you control it.

Who we are

NexivoReach is operated as a private / invite-only product deployment. “We” means the operators of the deployment you were invited to use.

What we collect

  • Account data — Google account email, name, and profile photo when you sign in with Google.
  • Workspace data — company profiles, catalogs, ICP settings, leads, outreach drafts, activity logs, and support tickets you create.
  • Connected Google services — if you connect Gmail and/or Google Sheets, we store OAuth tokens so we can send mail you approve and sync spreadsheets you authorize.
  • Usage metrics — daily counts of hunts, extracts, prepares, and sends for rate limits and ops.
  • Support content — ticket text, appeals, and any images you attach.

How we use data

  • To run discovery, scoring, drafting, sending, and Sheets sync that you request.
  • To enforce invite-only access, usage caps, suspensions, and team seats.
  • To operate admin tools for invited operators of this deployment.
  • To improve reliability (logs, error diagnosis) without selling your customer lists.

Google user data

Google sign-in uses basic profile scopes. Gmail and Sheets are connected only when you choose Workspace → Connect. We use Gmail only to send and read outreach threads you initiate, and Sheets only to create/update workbooks you authorize. We do not use Google data for advertising.

You can disconnect Gmail or Sheets anytime in Workspace → Connect; that clears our stored tokens for that service.

AI and third parties

Prospect research and drafting may send relevant product, ICP, or lead context to AI providers configured for this deployment (for example Groq or Gemini). Web search providers may receive search queries. Payment processors (if enabled) receive billing details you submit to them — we store plan status, not full card numbers.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is active on this deployment. You may request deletion via in-app Support (or the suspension appeal form if access is paused). Operators may delete accounts or clear leads on request. Support tickets may be retained briefly for review history.

Security

Access is protected by Google sign-in and session cookies. Tokens for Gmail/Sheets are stored on your user record for this deployment. Use a strong Google account security posture (2FA recommended).

Children

NexivoReach is a B2B tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use in-app Support, or email the operator who invited you.
